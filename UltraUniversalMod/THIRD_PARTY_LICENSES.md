UltraUniversalMod bundles six TrueType fonts. Their licenses are separate from the UltraUniversalMod LICENSE.

| `fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf` | DejaVu Sans; Bitstream, Inc., Tavmjong Bah, and DejaVu contributors. DejaVu changes are in the public domain. | [DejaVu Fonts](https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts) | [Bitstream Vera and Arev terms](licenses/DejaVuSans-LICENSE.txt) |

The font files' embedded metadata identifies these families, authors, and licenses. The linked full license texts are copied from the upstream projects. The exact download origin of the individual binary copies predates this release audit; their embedded metadata agrees with the cited upstream projects. All six font files may be redistributed with the mod when these notices and license texts remain included.
